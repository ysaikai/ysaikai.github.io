Privacy Policy for Gardening Assistant

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Information Collection and Use

We built this app with user privacy in mind. The app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any user data.
  • Local-Only Processing: The app will require you to enter a coarse location in which your garden is situated to obtain local weather data. This data and any other information you enter are processed entirely on your local device.
  • No Tracking: We do not track your location, browsing history, or app usage.
  • Data Synchronization: If you enable iCloud, your data is securely transmitted to and stored in your personal iCloud account to sync across your own devices. We (the developer) have no access to this data.

Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party advertising networks, analytics frameworks, or external databases. The only data transmission that occurs is the optional synchronization with Apple's secure iCloud infrastructure, which is governed entirely by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
